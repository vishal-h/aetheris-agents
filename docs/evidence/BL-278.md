| interval | manifest delta | what the boundary did |

re-pin-only boundary writes neither, and rewrites cells in place. Averaging the two would produce a
figure describing no boundary that has occurred. **Do not average them**, and do not treat either as
a rate: each is one observation of its own kind.

**As bounds, stated as such.** At the observed add-bearing cost of +775, the 3,809 bytes of headroom
is ~5 boundaries. At the observed re-pin-only cost of +234, ~16. Each rests on a single observation
of that kind, and neither is a projection: the mix of add-bearing and re-pin-only boundaries is not
something this row has measured.
