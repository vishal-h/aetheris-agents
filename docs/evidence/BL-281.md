# BL-281 — two documents still state the amend rule's superseded trigger

BL-280 changed the trigger of `CLAUDE.md` §Definition of done's amend rule from **citation** to
**push state**. Two documents restate the superseded trigger; neither is in BL-280's scope, and
one is in the sibling repo.

**1. Harness `docs/methodology/milestone-methodology.md:134`, ruling `[R44]`** — the load-bearing
one, because it does not merely quote the rule, it *derives* its own operative word from it:

> 15. **A commit held for review is pushed by a later session, unamended** `[R44]`. The session
> that wrote it does not push on its own judgement; the review is the gate, and a gate the author
> can walk through is not one. "Unamended" is `aetheris-agents/CLAUDE.md`'s amend-while-uncited
> rule applied to a commit a packet has cited.

A commit held for review is by definition **unpushed**, so the push-state trigger alone would
*permit* amending it — the opposite of what R44 imports from the superseded wording.

**The basis R44 needs now exists; nothing is left to decide.** BL-280's completing amendment added
the review-integrity limb to the agents rule: *once a packet has been issued against a commit, amend
it only on the arbiter's instruction*. R44's conclusion is unchanged and its basis is supplied — it
follows from that limb instead of from the superseded amend-while-uncited wording. **The remaining
work is restating the derivation at `:134`, not settling a question.** It is a harness-side edit,
outside BL-280's one agents commit.

**2. Agents `docs/milestones/hc-consolidation.md:922`, the dated note under `R22`** — quotes the

> SECOND, the choice between amending a commit and appending another is neither R32's question nor

superseded wording in the position it occupied — **not a silent edit**. Nothing here is undecided
either; it is the block that is owed. That is an edit to the R-number record, which the BL-280
ticket did not authorise.
